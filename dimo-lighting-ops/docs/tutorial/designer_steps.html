Lighting Designer / Engineer – Step by Step
Concepts, layouts, calculations, controls and electrical design – you see only the jobs assigned to you · https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app
Your flowJob assigned → Confirm due date → Work and log progress → Upload design files → Save brands → Submit for review → Answer clarifications. Button names are shown like this; menu paths like My Jobs → open a job.
Deadlines to watchConfirm or request another due date within 4 working hours · Log progress daily · Answer Estimation clarifications within 1 working day.

1A new job arrives

  1. You get a notification "Design job assigned". Your home screen My Jobs lists your jobs with their colour status.
  2. Open the job and, within 4 working hours, tap Confirm due date – or Request another date with a reason (the Design Manager decides).

2Understand the request

  1. Read Scope from sales and the Client expectation (solution level, preferred brands, origin, budget).
  2. Open the attached drawings and documents. Tap Open inquiry to see the full request.

3Work and log progress (daily)

In Work and log:

  1. Enter the Progress %, the Hours to log today, and tick the items in the Deliverables checklist.
  2. Tap Save progress.

4Upload the design files

Under Design files:

  1. Upload drafts as + Draft while you work.
  2. Lighting final set as + Design pack: layouts, calculation reports (DIALux / Relux), luminaire schedule, controls.
  3. Electrical final set: single-line diagram, DB and cable schedules, load and voltage-drop report, electrical BOQ.

5Brands and submit

  1. Under Brands specified in the design, enter the brand per product group and tap Save brands.
  2. Tap Submit for review. The Design Manager reviews within 1 working day.
  3. If returned, the comments appear at the top – update the files and submit again.

6Waiting for the client?

  1. Tap Put on hold, choose the reason and who you are waiting on. The clock pauses (grey).
  2. Tap Resume when the information arrives.

+Also yours

  • Clarifications from Estimation: questions from the estimator appear on the job. Tap Answer within 1 working day. Upload revised files as new versions – the estimator is notified.
  • Running late? On the inquiry page, your red stage timer has a Delay reason button. Enter the reason and a revised date – sales and SM Projects are informed.
  • Reports: your own job history, on-time % and hours.
