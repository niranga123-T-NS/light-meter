SM Projects – Step by Step
Senior Manager – Building & Infrastructure Projects: approvals, coaching and control of the sales team · https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app
Your flowApprove weekly plans → Confirm release modes on new inquiries → Review visits → Manage projects and customers → Watch the dashboard. Button names are shown like this; menu paths like Approvals → Weekly plans.
Deadlines to watchApprove weekly plans by Monday 09:00 · Confirm the release mode of every new inquiry promptly (design cannot be released until you do) · Answer approvals the same working day.

1Start of the day

  1. Your home screen is the Sales Management dashboard: delay control, customer deadlines at risk, pipeline by project type, inquiry funnel, SLA performance, sales activity per person, top overdue items and debtors. Tap any number to open the records behind it.
  2. Open the Approvals tab – everything waiting for you is listed there.

2Approve weekly plans (by Monday 09:00)

Plans → open a plan waiting for approval.

  1. Check the planned visits, objectives and customers. Tender visits are highlighted.
  2. Tap Approve, Approve with comments, or Return for changes (the sales person corrects and resubmits the same day).
  3. If a sales person planned a visit to another person's customer, use Duplicate…: approve as a joint visit, reassign the account, or reject the visit.

3Confirm the release mode of each new inquiry

Every new inquiry brings a release-mode approval to your Approvals tab.

  1. Mode 3 – design and quotation released together (usual for Route A). Mode 2 – quotation only, design stays internal. Mode 1 – design only (Route C).
  2. Approve the proposed mode, or return it with a reason. The Design Manager cannot release a design until you confirm.

4Other approvals

  1. Debtor check: approve to let the inquiry proceed, or reject to keep it on hold (ask for a collection plan).
  2. Mixed duty offers: first approval (GM / DGM gives the final one).
  3. Duty status change, release mode change, early design release (2nd step): approve or return with a reason.
  4. Sample requests / new return dates: open the sample → Approve, Return with comment or Reject.

5Coach the team

  1. Visit review: Visits → To review → open a visit → Mark reviewed / add coaching comment. Visits far from the site show a GPS review.
  2. Plan evaluation: on an approved plan see plan-vs-actual (completion %, objective match %, GPS-verified %, missed reasons) and tap Rate the week.
  3. Scorecards: choose a sales person and month → set targets (sent to GM / DGM) and add a review comment.

6Projects and customers

  1. Create a project: Projects → + New project – fill it in as sales would, and choose the Sales person and First visit due under Assignment. The sales person is notified.
  2. On a project: Reassign sales person, override Win probability with a comment, or Merge a duplicate into this.
  3. Customers: change the account owner of an organization or unit (reason required). The client view shows projects by status and every pending design / estimation.

+Also yours

  • Sales person leaving: Team & lists → Users → Transfer accounts – customers, open projects, inquiries, plan lines and debts move in one step; history stays with the original person.
  • Competitors: add new names under Team & lists → Competitors.
  • You receive delay alerts for every sales inquiry, dormant-project and late-plan alerts, and debt alerts at 60, 120 and 180 days.
