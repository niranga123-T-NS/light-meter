SM Estimation – Step by Step
Senior Manager – Estimation: accepts estimation work, assigns estimators, approves and releases quotations · https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app
Your flowInquiry (Route B) or design released from Design (Route A) → Accept → Assign estimator → Estimator prices → Approve quotation → Release to sales. Button names are shown like this; menu paths like Jobs → To accept / assign.
Deadlines to watchAccept / assign within 4 working hours · Approve a quotation within 1 working day · Leave at least 1 working day before the customer deadline · Estimator hold requests within 4 working hours.

1Start of the day – the Estimation Board

  1. Your home screen is the Estimation Board: tabs Queue (from Design and direct Route B) · In progress · Approval · Released.
  2. It also shows workload per estimator and jobs waiting for supplier prices. Deal with red and amber items first.

2Route B – estimation-only inquiry

Open the inquiry from the Queue.

  1. Check the BOQ / specification, Duty status, dates and the client solution expectation.
  2. All good: tap Accept.
  3. BOQ or specification missing – design needed: tap Send to Design (Route A) with a reason. It becomes Route A and goes to the Design Manager; sales is notified.
  4. Information missing: tap Return for information (the clock pauses). Not valid: tap Reject with a reason.

3Route A – design released from Design

When the Design Manager taps Release design to Estimation, the job arrives in your Queue.

  1. Open the estimation job, check the Inputs (request documents and approved design pack) and tap Accept.

4Assign the estimator

On the estimation job tap Assign estimator.

  1. The default is pre-selected: Assistant Manager – Estimation for Infrastructure / Industrial; Estimation Executive for Hospitality, Retail, Institutions and Commercial. Choosing the other person needs a reason.
  2. Set the value band and the due date – it must leave at least 1 working day before the customer deadline.
  3. Save. The estimator is notified and confirms the due date.

5Follow the work

  1. The estimator prices the job, raises + Supplier price request when needed, and asks Design through Clarifications to Design.
  2. Due date changes: Change due date (reason logged). Workload changes: Reassign / re-plan. Waiting on something: Put on hold / Resume.
  3. Approvals tab: estimator hold requests (answer within 4 working hours).

6Approve the quotation (within 1 working day)

When the estimator taps Submit for approval, the job moves to Approval.

  1. Open the job: check the draft quotation PDF, the restricted costing sheet, Quoted value, Cost and Margin %, Validity and alternatives.
  2. Correct: tap Approve quotation. Changes needed: tap Return with comments.
  3. Above the value limit or below the margin floor, approval goes automatically to GM / DGM as a second step.

7Release to sales

After final approval:

  1. Tap Release to sales. The quotation files are renamed with the quotation number and the sales person is notified.
  2. Sales then records the submission to the client and the result (won / lost).

+Also yours

  • Approvals tab: client expectation changes (together with the Design Manager).
  • You are notified when a design is sent to Estimation, when the client changes the duty status, and on Level 2 escalations for your team.
  • Reports: Estimation team performance, Quotation register, Tender and competitor report.
