Operations Executive – Step by Step
Weekly debtors upload, legal status of debts and the samples queue · https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app
Your flowSaturday: upload the debtors list → During the week: legal cases and collection mismatches → Samples: check availability, dispatch, record return. Button names are shown like this; menu paths like Debtors Upload → Choose Excel file.
Deadlines to watchDebtors upload every Saturday (reminder at 16:00; if nothing is uploaded by 22:00, SM Projects and GM / DGM are alerted on Monday 09:00) · Sample availability check within 1 working day · Legal hearing updates the day after each hearing.

1Your home screen

  1. Menu: Home · Debtors Upload · All Debtors · Samples · Approvals · Reports.
  2. Home shows the last upload, Legal cases, Collection mismatches, Hearing outcome to enter, and the samples queue: Availability to check, Approved – to dispatch, Out – due for return.
  3. You do not see visits, pipeline, inquiries, design, estimation or scorecards.

2Saturday – upload the debtors list

Debtors Upload:

  1. First time only: tap Download template. Columns: Project name, Client, Invoice number, Invoice date, Outstanding amount, Currency (LKR / USD), Outstanding days, Sales person (optional).
  2. Fill the template from the accounts system (one row per invoice) and save it.
  3. Set As at (the statement date) and tap Choose Excel file. The system checks every row and shows a Preview with totals and errors.
  4. Rows marked Project not matched: tap Map and pick the right project. Fix any other errors in Excel and upload again.
  5. When there are no errors, tap Confirm upload. New invoices are added, amounts and days are updated, and invoices no longer in the file are cleared.
  6. Sales people and managers are alerted automatically when debts cross 60, 120 and 180 days.

3Legal cases (only you can set Legal)

Open the debt from All Debtors:

  1. Tap Place under Legal: a short description (max 180 characters – case, court, lawyer, stage) and the next hearing date.
  2. Two days before each hearing, GM / DGM, SM Projects and you receive a critical notification.
  3. The day after a hearing: tap Update legal / next hearing with the new date, or Close legal case with the outcome.

4Collection mismatches

  1. A debt marked collected by sales but still in your latest file is listed under All Debtors → Collection mismatch.
  2. Check with accounts and the sales person, then correct it in the next upload.

5Samples – check availability (within 1 working day)

Open the request from Availability to check:

  1. Tap Record availability: Available, Partly available or Not available (with the reason or expected date).
  2. SM Projects then approves or rejects the request.

6Samples – dispatch and handover

From Approved – to dispatch:

  1. Arrange dispatch to the Handover location by the Required at site by date.
  2. Under Documents and delivery note, add + Delivery note / handover photo (signed delivery note or photo).
  3. Tap Record handover with the handover person. The expected return date starts counting.

7Samples – return

From Out – due for return:

  1. When a returnable sample comes back, tap Record return with its condition: Good, Damaged or Incomplete.
  2. Damaged or lost samples are flagged to SM Projects. Overdue returns are reminded to the sales person automatically.

+Also yours

  • Upload history: every past upload with its totals, under Debtors Upload.
  • Reports → Debtors ageing report – export to PDF or Excel for management.
