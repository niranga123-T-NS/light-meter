Getting started
For everyone

1.1 Sign in

  1. Open https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  2. Enter your work email and the temporary password from the System Administrator, then tap Sign in.
  3. Change the password straight away: your name (bottom-left) → Profile → Change password. Type the new password twice (at least 8 characters) and save.
Make it an app icon iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. A DIMO icon then opens the system directly.

Forgot your password? Type your email on the sign-in page and tap Forgot password, or ask the System Administrator to reset it.

1.2 The screen

Computer / tablet A dark menu on the left shows only what your role may use. Your picture and name are at the bottom – tap them for your Profile.
Phone A bottom bar shows your four main screens. More opens everything else, plus Notifications, Search and Sign out.

Every screen has two icons at the top right:

  • Search (magnifier) – finds customers, units, contacts, projects, inquiries, quotation numbers, tender numbers and invoice numbers. Partial words work. You only ever see records you are allowed to see.
  • Bell – your notifications. A red number shows how many are unread. Tap a notification to open the record it is about.

1.3 Your profile

  • Profile picture: Profile → Upload picture (or Take photo on a phone). JPG or PNG up to 5 MB. Without a picture your initials are shown on a coloured circle.
  • Daily digest: tick "Daily digest at 08:30 instead of individual non-critical notices" if you prefer one summary a day. Critical alerts always arrive immediately.
  • Your name, role and reporting manager are maintained by the System Administrator.

1.4 Things that work the same everywhere

  • Reasons are required whenever you return, reject, put on hold, change a date or go outside a rule. The system asks for them in a pop-up; everything is logged with your name and the time.
  • Approvals tab (managers): every item waiting for your decision, in one list. Approve, Return or Reject – returning and rejecting need a reason.
  • Files: use the + … buttons in the Files sections to attach drawings, BOQs, photos and PDFs (up to 50 MB each). Every version is kept. Open a file with Open / Download – downloads are logged.
  • Reports tab: shows only the reports your role may run. Set the filters, tap Run, then Export PDF or Export Excel. PDFs carry the DIMO header and a watermark with your name and the time.
  • Currencies: Duty Free = USD and Duty Paid = LKR. Every value shows its currency; totals show both, plus an LKR total at the month's exchange rate.

1.5 Status colours

ColourMeaning
GreenOn time – less than 75% of the allowed time used.
AmberAt risk – 75% used, or less than 1 working day left.
RedOverdue – the due time has passed. Delayed items are pinned to the top of lists.
GreyOn hold – the clock is paused and the hold reason is shown.

Clocks count working hours only: Monday–Friday 08:30–17:30, excluding Sri Lanka public and mercantile holidays.

2.1 The three routes

RouteUse whenGoes toEnds with
A · Design → EstimationA layout, concept, calculation or electrical design is needed before pricingDesign Manager, then SM EstimationQuotation (and design) released to sales
B · Estimation onlyBOQ or specification already available (tender BOQ, consultant spec, replacement)SM EstimationQuotation released to sales
C · Design onlyConcept, calculation or presentation support, no quotation yetDesign ManagerDesign pack returned to sales

2.2 Release modes (confirmed by SM Projects)

ModeDesign goes toClient receives
1 · Design onlySales personDesign pack only
2 · Estimation onlyEstimation team only (internal input)Quotation only
3 · Design + Estimation (default for route A)Estimation first; design is held until the quotation is releasedDesign and quotation together. An early design release needs Design Manager then SM Projects approval.

2.3 Inquiry stages you will see

Draft → Submitted → Accepted → In design → Design review → Design approved → In estimation → Estimation review → Quotation released → Submitted to client → (Awaiting client approval → Client approved) → Won / Lost / On hold / Cancelled.

Route B skips the design stages; route C ends at Design returned to sales. At every stage the inquiry shows one current owner and one due date, a progress bar and its colour.

Who sees what Each team sees only its own part. Sales see a progress tracker (stage, %, dates, colour) for work in Design and Estimation – not drafts, hours, cost or margin – and download only the released design pack and quotation. Design sees only inquiries routed to Design (designers only their own jobs). Estimation sees only inquiries routed to it plus the approved design pack. GM / DGM sees everything.
3
Assistant Sales Managers
Building Lighting (Hospitality, Retail, Institutions, Commercial) · Infrastructure Lighting (Infrastructure, Industrial)

Your menu: My Day · Visits · Plan · Projects · Pending · Customers · My Debtors · Samples · Scorecard · Reports.

3.1 My Day

Your home screen shows today's planned visits (with a Check in button), next actions due, pending designs and estimations, delayed items, quotations awaiting follow-up, dormant projects, projects whose win probability has not been reviewed in 30 days, new projects assigned to you, open debts and overdue samples. A yellow note reminds you if next week's plan is not submitted.

3.2 Plan next week (submit by Saturday 13:00)

  1. Plan → Open next week's plan.
  2. Tap + Add visit. Choose the day and time slot, Normal or Tender (then the tender activity), the project, the organization / unit / contact, the Visit Category and the planned objective. Tap Add to plan. Repeat for every visit.
  3. Tap Submit plan for approval. SM Projects approves it by Monday 09:00, or returns it – then change it and resubmit the same day.
  4. During the week use Reschedule, Cancel or Missed on a planned visit. A reason is required and every change is logged.
Reminders and alertsYou are reminded on Friday 16:00 and Saturday 10:00. A plan submitted after Saturday 13:00 is marked Late and SM Projects is alerted. Planning a visit to a customer owned by the other sales person also alerts SM Projects.

3.3 Check in to a visit

  1. From My Day tap Check in on a planned visit (details are filled in), or tap Check in to a visit for an unplanned one.
  2. Allow location access – your GPS position is captured only at check-in and check-out. It is never tracked continuously.
  3. Choose Normal or Tender, the project (only networking visits may have no project), the customer, the Visit Category, then the objective group and primary objective (up to 3 secondary objectives).
  4. Either tap Check in (report later), or fill the report now and tap Save visit report.

The visit report (same day, by 20:00)

  • Discussion summary – at least 30 characters (use the keyboard microphone for voice-to-text), and the Outcome.
  • Competitors mentioned, brands specified, estimated project and lighting values, next action and date (you get a reminder on that date).
  • On the visit page tap Check out and save report. Then add business cards, drawings and site photos under Photos and documents.
  • If the objective signals progress or risk (e.g. Specification Inclusion, Brand Approval, Price Negotiation) the app asks you to confirm the project's win probability.
No signal?Save the visit anyway. It is kept on your phone ("waiting to sync") and sent automatically when you are back online, with the original check-in time.

Tender visits

For a Bid Submission or Tender Opening visit you must complete the Tender result form on the visit page before checking out: tender no. and name, type, duty status, dates, our price and brands, each competitor's bid (compliant or not). The system shows our rank (L1, L2 …) and the gap to L1. The result updates the project automatically.

3.4 Customers and projects

  • New customer: Customers → + New. Organization, type, contact details, optional first unit and contact. Possible duplicates (same name, phone or email) are shown – use the existing one if it is the same customer. You become the account owner.
  • Units and contacts: on the customer page, + Unit (up to 3 levels, e.g. division → branch → site) and + Contact.
  • New project: Projects → + New project. Name it Customer – Project – City, choose the type and customer, set values and the expected duration in months (mandatory – the term Short / Medium / Long is suggested). If similar projects exist, tap Use this project or confirm it is different and give a reason. Exact duplicates are blocked.
  • On the project page: Win probability (the stage proposes a default; outside the band you must give a reason), Stage, Specification, Lighting value, Award date, Term / duration, Review status (active, on hold with review date, lost, cancelled, handed over). The stakeholder map shows which categories have been met.
  • Dormant projects: after 60 days without activity a project is flagged Dormant – review it within 5 working days.

3.5 Raise an inquiry

  1. From a visit tap Convert to inquiry (or Pending → + New inquiry).
  2. Choose the route (A / B / C), release mode, design scope, duty status (mandatory for routes A and B – it fixes USD or LKR), priority, customer deadline (mandatory), your required-by dates, the scope, the documents-received checklist and the client's solution level and origin.
  3. Tap Save draft and continue. Use Save and add another inquiry for a second package from the same visit.
  4. On the inquiry page attach drawings, BOQ and specifications under Inquiry documents, then tap Submit inquiry.
The system may stop you Mixed duty: if the project already has an offer with the other duty status, an approval goes to SM Projects then GM / DGM – submit again once approved.
Debtor check: if the client has debts over 90 days or under legal action you see a warning, and SM Projects must allow the inquiry.
Time: you are warned if the time to the customer deadline is shorter than the standard turnaround.

3.6 Follow your inquiries – the Pending tab

Tabs: Pending designs · Pending estimations · All open · Delayed · Ready / with client · Drafts / returned · Closed. Each card shows stage, progress, internal due date, customer deadline, days left and colour. Delayed items are pinned to the top in red with the delay reason and revised date. You receive a push when a stage becomes overdue, every working morning while it stays overdue, and when it is completed.

On the inquiry page you can also:

  • Customer extended the deadline – new date and reason; Design / Estimation are notified.
  • Request a change – duty status, client solution level / origin, release mode, or an early design release. Each goes for approval.
  • Tap a red Stage timer only if you own it; otherwise just watch the tracker.

3.7 After release – client submission and result

  1. You get a push "Quotation released" (or "Design released"). Download the files from Released design pack and quotation files.
  2. Send them to the client yourself (the system never contacts clients), then tap Record submission to client with the date – within 1 working day.
  3. For designs sent for approval tap Record client response: Approved, Approved with comments, or Revision required (a new revision R1, R2 … goes back to Design).
  4. When decided tap Record result: Won (order value and date), Lost (reason and competitor), On hold or Cancelled.

Quotations are valid 30 days – you are reminded 7 days before expiry. Follow-up reminders arrive every 7 days until a result is recorded.

3.8 My Debtors

Debts on your projects appear after the Saturday upload, coloured by age (1–30 green … over 365 black). You receive one short push per open debt every other day. Open a debt and tap Update status: Follow-up done (note + next date), Payment promised (date), Partially collected (amount), Collected (date) or Disputed (reason). "Collected" is confirmed automatically when the invoice disappears from the next upload.

3.9 Samples

  1. Samples → + Request sample: project, returnable or not (return date), purpose, required-by date, items with quantities and values, handover location and person.
  2. Submit request → Operations checks availability → SM Projects approves → Operations hands it over.
  3. Returnable samples: you are reminded every other working day after the return date. Need longer? Request new return date (SM Projects approves).

3.10 Scorecard and reports

Scorecard shows your monthly score out of 100 across order intake, pipeline, activity, win rate and coverage, with every KPI and the change from last month. When SM Projects adds a review comment, tap Acknowledge. Reports gives your own visit, pipeline, tender, quotation, stakeholder, debtors and samples reports.

4.2 Coaching and control

  • Plan evaluation: on an approved plan see plan-vs-actual (completion %, objective match %, GPS-verified %, missed reasons) and tap Rate the week.
  • Visit review: Visits → To review → open a visit → Mark reviewed / add coaching comment. Visits far from the site show GPS review.
  • Projects: create projects and assign a sales person (they are notified and get a first-visit action), Reassign sales person, override Win probability with a comment, and Merge a duplicate into this.
  • Customers: change the account owner of an organization or a unit (reason required); the client view shows projects by status and every pending design / estimation with overdue items flagged.
  • Transfer accounts when a sales person leaves: Team & lists → Users → Transfer accounts. Customers, units, open projects, inquiries, plan lines and debts move in one step; history stays with the original person.
  • Competitors: add new competitor names under Team & lists → Competitors.
  • Scorecards: choose a sales person and month → Targets (sent to GM / DGM) and Add review comment.

4.3 Sales Management dashboard

Delay control, customer deadlines at risk, pipeline by project type (weighted, dormant excluded), inquiry funnel, SLA performance, sales activity per person, top overdue items and the debtors summary. Tap any number to open the records behind it. You also receive delay alerts for every sales inquiry, dormant-project and late-plan alerts, and debt crossings at 60, 120 and 180 days.

Design Manager
Accepts design inquiries, assigns designers, reviews and releases designs

Your menu: Design Board · Approvals · Inquiries · Jobs · Reports.

6.1 Design Board

Summary tiles (open, due this week, overdue, in review), workload by designer (open jobs, overdue, hours logged) and columns: To accept / assign · Assigned · In progress · On hold · In review · Approved / released.

6.2 Accept and assign (4 working hours each)

  1. Open an inquiry in To accept / assign. Check the scope and documents, then Accept, Return for information (clock pauses, sales notified) or Reject.
  2. Tap Assign designer: task (Lighting, or Electrical – Lighting Engineer only), assignee, job size (small 3 wd, medium 5 wd, large 10 wd) and the due date. For Lighting + Electrical scope assign two tasks.
  3. If your due date is later than the sales-requested date, choose a reason – sales and SM Projects are notified.

6.3 Review and release

  1. When a designer submits, the job moves to In review (1 working day). Open it: Approve design or Return with comments (counted as a review cycle).
  2. When every task is approved the inquiry shows Design approved. On the inquiry tap Release design to Estimation (modes 2 and 3) or Release design to sales (mode 1). The release button is disabled until SM Projects confirms the release mode.
  3. If the brands do not match the client's stated level or origin you must give a justification.

6.4 Other actions on a design job

  • Change due date (versioned with reason – also used to accept a designer's date-change request), Reassign (clock continues), Put on hold / Resume.
  • Approvals tab: first step of early design release requests and client expectation changes.
  • Clarifications from Estimation unanswered for 1 working day are flagged to you.
  • Reports → Design team performance: jobs completed, on-time %, turnaround, review cycles, hours.
7
Lighting Designer and Lighting Engineer
Concepts, layouts, calculations, controls and electrical design

Your menu: My Jobs · All my jobs · Reports. You see only the jobs assigned to you.

7.1 Working a design job

  1. You get a push "Design job assigned". Open the job and, within 4 working hours, tap Confirm due date – or Request another date with a reason.
  2. Read Scope from sales and open the attached drawings.
  3. As you work, enter the progress %, hours for today and tick milestones, then tap Save progress.
  4. Upload drafts under Design files (+ Draft) and the final set as + Design pack: layouts, calculation reports (DIALux / Relux), luminaire schedule, controls; for electrical: single-line diagram, DB and cable schedules, load and voltage-drop report, electrical BOQ.
  5. Enter the Brands specified per product group and tap Save brands.
  6. Tap Submit for review. If returned, the comments appear at the top – update and submit again.
Waiting for the client?Tap Put on hold, choose the reason and who you are waiting on. The clock pauses (grey). Tap Resume when the information arrives.

7.2 Clarifications

Questions from the estimator appear under Clarifications from Estimation. Tap Answer within 1 working day. Upload revised files as new versions – the estimator is notified.

7.3 If you are late

On the inquiry page, the red stage timer you own has a Delay reason button. Enter the reason and a revised date – sales and SM Projects are informed. The item stays red on dashboards until it is completed.

9
Assistant Manager – Estimation and Estimation Executive
BOQ, costing, technical and commercial proposals

Your menu: My Estimates · All my jobs · Reports. You see only the jobs assigned to you.

  1. You get a push "Estimate assigned". Open it and tap Confirm due date (or Request another date) within 4 working hours. The header shows Duty Free · USD or Duty Paid · LKR – price in that currency. You cannot change it.
  2. Open the inputs: the request documents and the approved design pack. Ask the designer with + Ask under Clarifications to Design – never by email.
  3. Enter the quoted value, cost (restricted – sales never see it), margin %, validity (default 30 days), alternatives, and the brands offered per product group. Log supplier price requests with + Supplier price request. Tap Save estimate.
  4. Upload the Draft quotation (PDF) and Costing sheet (Excel), then tap Submit for approval. Files are renamed automatically, e.g. QTN-2026-00125-R0.pdf.
  5. When approved, upload the Final quotation PDF, Compliance sheet and Technical data sheets, then tap Release to sales. If brands do not match the client's expectation, give a justification.
Waiting on a supplier?Waiting for prices does not pause the clock. If you really need a hold tap Request hold – SM Estimation must approve it.

Client revisions create a new quotation revision (R1, R2 …) with its own timeline; earlier versions are kept.

Operations Executive
Weekly debtors upload, legal status and samples

Your menu: Home · Debtors Upload · All Debtors · Samples · Approvals · Reports. You do not see visits, pipeline, inquiries, design, estimation or scorecards.

10.1 Saturday debtors upload

  1. Debtors Upload → Download template (first time only). Columns: Project name, Client, Invoice number, Invoice date, Outstanding amount, Currency (LKR / USD), Outstanding days, Sales person (optional).
  2. Set As at and tap Choose Excel file. The system checks every row and shows a preview with totals and errors.
  3. For rows marked Project not matched tap Map and pick the right project. Fix other errors in Excel and upload again.
  4. When there are no errors tap Confirm upload. New invoices are added, amounts and days updated, and invoices no longer in the file are cleared.
DeadlineYou are reminded on Saturday at 16:00. If nothing is uploaded by Saturday 22:00, SM Projects and GM / DGM are alerted on Monday 09:00.

10.2 Legal cases (only you can set Legal)

Open a debt → Place under Legal: a short description (max 180 characters – case, court, lawyer, stage) and the next hearing date. Two days before each hearing GM / DGM, SM Projects and you receive a critical push. The day after a hearing, update the next hearing date or tap Close legal case with the outcome.

Collection mismatches (marked collected by sales but still in your file) are listed under All Debtors → Collection mismatch.

10.3 Samples queue

  1. Availability check (1 working day): open the request → Record availability – Available, Partly available or Not available (with the reason or expected date).
  2. After SM Projects approves, arrange dispatch. Attach a photo or signed delivery note (+ Delivery note / handover photo), then Record handover.
  3. When a returnable sample comes back: Record return with its condition (Good, Damaged, Incomplete). Damaged or lost samples are flagged to SM Projects.

Deadlines, colours, escalations and notifications
For everyone

12.1 Standard turnaround times (working time)

StageTarget
Visit reportSame day, by 20:00
Inquiry acceptance · Assignment · Assignee acknowledgement4 working hours each
Design – small / medium / large or tender3 / 5 / 10 working days (or manager-set)
Design review by the Design Manager1 working day
Estimation – small (< LKR 5 M) / medium (5–25 M) / large (> 25 M)2 / 4 / 7 working days (or manager-set)
Quotation approval · Sales submission to client · Approvals1 working day each
Quotation follow-upEvery 7 days until a result

A due date set by a manager always overrides the standard; the standard is used to warn when a date is unrealistic.

12.2 Escalation ladder (applies to every stage)

WhenWho is notified
Work assignedAssignee
75% of the time usedAssignee
1 working day before dueAssignee + their manager
Due time passed – Level 1+ requesting sales person + SM Projects
Overdue 1 working day – Level 2+ Senior Manager of the team (SM Projects or SM Estimation)
Overdue 2 working days – Level 3 (critical)+ GM / DGM
Customer deadline within 2 days, not released (critical)Sales person, managers in the chain, GM / DGM

Overdue notices repeat every working morning at 09:00 until the item moves. The owner enters a delay reason and revised date (the Delay reason button on the stage timer).

12.3 Notifications

  • Internal only: push notifications on the phone app, and the bell list and browser notifications on the web. Nothing is ever sent to clients or consultants, and no email or WhatsApp is used.
  • Quiet hours: non-critical notices are held between 20:00 and 07:00 and on Sundays and public holidays, then delivered together at 07:00. Critical items arrive immediately.
  • Important unopened items (overdue, approvals, critical) are re-sent once after 2 working hours and stay pinned at the top of your list until opened.
  • Daily digest at 08:30: what is due today and what is overdue for you.

Quick answers
Frequently asked questions
QuestionAnswer
I can't see a menu, report or record a colleague can see.Each role sees only its own part (chapter 2). If your role is wrong, ask the System Administrator.
"Your account has no profile yet."Your login exists but no role is assigned – ask the System Administrator.
A button is grey or missing.The record is not at the right stage, or it belongs to someone else. The timeline on the inquiry shows where it is.
Why can't I edit my submitted inquiry?After submission, changes go through Request a change or Customer extended the deadline so everything is logged.
Why was my inquiry put on hold straight away?The client has debts over 90 days or under legal action. SM Projects decides whether it proceeds.
"Win probability … is outside the band – give a reason".Each stage has an allowed range (e.g. Brand specified 40–65%). Enter a reason to go outside it.
My visit says "waiting to sync".It was saved without signal. It syncs automatically when you are online; you can also use Profile → Sync now.
