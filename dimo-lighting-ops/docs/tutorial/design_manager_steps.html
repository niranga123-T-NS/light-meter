Design Manager – Step by Step
Accepts design inquiries (Route A and C), assigns designers, reviews and releases designs · https://dimo-lighting-ops.vercel.app
Your flowNew inquiry (Route A or C) → Accept → Assign designer → Designer works → Review → Release design. Button names are shown like this; menu paths like Jobs → To accept / assign.
Deadlines to watchAccept / assign each new inquiry within 4 working hours · Review a submitted design within 1 working day · Answer Estimation clarifications within 1 working day.

1Start of the day – the Design Board

  1. Your home screen is the Design Board: tiles for open, due this week, overdue and in review, plus workload by designer (open jobs, overdue, hours logged).
  2. Columns: To accept / assign · Assigned · In progress · On hold · In review · Approved / released. Red and amber cards need action first.
  3. You are notified (app and email) as soon as a sales person submits a Route A or C inquiry.

2Check and accept a new inquiry

Open the inquiry from To accept / assign.

  1. Check the Scope, Design scope, Client solution expectation, dates and the Inquiry documents (drawings, BOQ, RCPs, photos).
  2. All good: tap Accept.
  3. Information missing: tap Return for information and say what is needed – the clock pauses and the sales person is notified.
  4. Not a valid request: tap Reject with a reason.

3Assign the designer

On the accepted inquiry tap Assign designer.

  1. Task: Lighting, or Electrical (Lighting Engineer only). For Lighting + Electrical scope, assign two tasks.
  2. Assign to: choose the designer – check the workload on the Design Board first.
  3. Job size: small (3 working days), medium (5) or large (10). The Design due date is suggested from this.
  4. If your due date is later than the date sales asked for, choose a Reason for later date – sales and SM Projects are notified.
  5. Tap Assign. The designer is notified and the job moves to Assigned.

4Follow the work

  1. The designer confirms the due date, logs hours and progress, and ticks the Deliverables checklist.
  2. Date-change request from the designer: open the job → Change due date (reason is logged; each change is a new version).
  3. Other actions on the job: Reassign (the clock continues), Put on hold / Resume (the clock pauses while on hold).

5Review the design (within 1 working day)

When the designer taps Submit for review, the job moves to In review.

  1. Open the job: check the Design files, the Deliverables checklist and the Brands specified in the design against the client expectation.
  2. Correct: tap Approve design.
  3. Changes needed: tap Return with comments – this counts as a review cycle and goes back to the designer.
  4. If the brands do not match the client's stated level or origin, you must give a justification.

6Release the design

When every task is approved the inquiry shows Design approved. Open the inquiry:

  1. Route A (mode 3 or 2): tap Release design to Estimation. SM Estimation is notified.
  2. Route C (mode 1, design only): tap Release design to sales. The sales person is notified.
  3. The release button stays disabled until SM Projects confirms the release mode.

+Also yours

  • Clarifications from Estimation: open the design job and tap Answer – unanswered after 1 working day they are flagged to you.
  • Approvals tab: first step of early design release requests and client expectation changes.
  • Reports → Design team performance: jobs completed, on-time %, turnaround, review cycles and hours.
