SRI LANKA CUSTOMS
Sri Lanka Customs is one of the oldest government departments, established in year 1806 and is mainly responsible for the collection of revenue and the enforcement of law
VISION
Secure and Prosperous nation through strong borders
MISSION
Providing world class customs service to secure revenue, protect environment and society and promote the seamless flow of trade and travel, with integrity and professionalism
OUR FUNCTIONS
  • Collection of Revenue
  • Prevention of Revenue Leakage and Other Frauds
  • Facilitation of Legitimate Trade
  • Collection of Import and Export Data to Provide Statistics
  • Cooperation and Coordination with Other Government Agencies

Sri Lanka Customs

Sri Lanka Customs is one of the oldest government departments, established in 1806. With the introduction of the Customs Ordinance, it evolved into a fully-fledged state institution responsible for revenue collection, trade facilitation, border protection, and enforcement of customs laws.

Mr. Wimal S. K. Liyanagama
Director General of Customs
