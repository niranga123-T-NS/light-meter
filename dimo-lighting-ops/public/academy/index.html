Watch out

Ask which chip brand is inside (for example Lumileds, Nichia, ams OSRAM, Cree, Samsung, Seoul) and for the LM-80 / TM-21 report. A cheap fitting often uses an unbranded chip driven hard on a thin heat sink – bright on day one, dim and patchy within two years.

In one line

KNX is the open world standard (ISO/IEC 14543) for building control – lights, blinds, heating, AC and security on one green bus cable. There is no central computer: every device has its own small brain and they talk with group addresses.
2

See it – press a button and follow the telegram

3

How KNX is built

Bus cableGreen twisted pair, 30 V DC safety extra-low voltage, 9600 bit/s (also KNX IP and KNX RF wireless)
LineUp to 64 devices on one power supply; more with line repeaters
TopologyLines → areas through couplers: 15 areas × 15 lines – tens of thousands of devices in one building
AddressesPhysical address (where the device is, e.g. 1.1.2) and group addresses (what it does, e.g. 1/0/1 “Office lights”)
ProgrammingOne tool for all brands: ETS, by a KNX-certified integrator
LightingSwitch / dimming actuators, or a KNX–DALI gateway to drive DALI drivers
4

Say this to the client

“KNX is an open standard with hundreds of manufacturers – you are never locked to one brand, and the same system runs your lights, blinds and AC. For the lighting itself we connect DALI drivers through a KNX–DALI gateway.”

Watch out

Open standards (KNX, DALI-2, BACnet, Modbus) let any qualified integrator maintain the system later. Proprietary systems (C-Bus, Lutron, Dynalite, Crestron…) can be excellent but tie the client to one brand and its partners for spares and changes – say so up front and price the service contract.
